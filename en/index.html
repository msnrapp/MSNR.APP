Yaroukana

Never forget the things you keep meaning to do.

What is it?

Yaroukana ("maybe I should do it" in Japanese) tracks the recurring upkeep of everyday life. It's not a calendar and not a todo list: you simply record when you did something, and the app works out when it's due next.

  • 🛁 Bathroom mold cleaning, air-conditioner filters
  • 🚗 Oil changes, tire pressure, vehicle inspection
  • 🦷 Dental checkups, health checkups, toothbrush replacement
  • 🐾 Pet vaccinations, nail trimming, grooming
  • 🌱 Watering, fertilizing, repotting plants
  • 🪪 License renewals, passports, emergency food rotation

Features

📅 Auto-calculated from the day you did it

Just tap "Done" - the next due date is calculated from your cycle. Fixed-date schedules (like license renewals) are supported too.

🔔 Reminders

Get notified when something is coming up. Notifications are generated entirely on your device - nothing is sent to any server.

🔒 Your data stays on your device

No account needed. Everything is stored locally, and no personal information ever leaves your phone.

✅ Templates to get started fast

Common chores for home, car, health and pets come as templates with sensible default cycles - add them in a few taps.
